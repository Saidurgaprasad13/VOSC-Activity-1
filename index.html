How to Play

  • 🎲 Roll the dice.
  • 6 is required to bring a token onto the board.
  • Click a highlighted token to move it.
  • Landing on an opponent sends their token home.
  • ⭐ Star cells are safe cells.
  • Reach the finish to complete a token.
  • Complete all 4 tokens to win.
